# Cases

Cases teach transferable business judgment and communication patterns. They are not current-customer evidence.

## Golden Cases
Store anonymized, abstract, reusable examples by business conflict/objective rather than by product name or customer identity.

## Anti-patterns
Store recurring failure modes such as over-official tone, over-structuring simple tasks, unsupported claims, mechanical follow-up, and unauthorized commitments.

## Usage rule
- Match cases by conflict, objective, and project stage.
- Never copy case facts into the current project.
- Prefer reasoning pattern over wording imitation.
