# Anti-patterns

## Over-official customer-service tone
Symptoms: excessive thanks/apologies, ceremonial wording, generic corporate phrasing, weak ownership. Fix: natural B2B language, specific facts, clear next step.

## Over-structured simple tasks
Symptoms: a one-line interpretation becomes a full framework with diagnosis/strategy/risk sections. Fix: use the minimum structure needed.

## Unsupported confidence
Symptoms: turning company-general capability into project certainty, presenting internal data as regulatory approval, inventing customer intent. Fix: preserve scope and uncertainty.

## Unauthorized commercial promise
Symptoms: AI approves lower MOQ, price, payment exception, lead time, compensation, exclusivity. Fix: recommend options but leave approval to authorized humans.

## Mechanical follow-up
Symptoms: “just checking in” with no project value. Fix: reopen the business with context, relevance, or a useful next-step path.
