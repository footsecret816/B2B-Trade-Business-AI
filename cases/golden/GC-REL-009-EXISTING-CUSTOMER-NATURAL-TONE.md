# GC-REL-009 — Existing Customer, Natural Operational Update

## Scenario
An existing customer receives a routine project update such as shipment status, artwork revision, balance invoice, sample progress, or a small order confirmation.

## Recommended reasoning pattern
1. Preserve the relationship context: this is an ongoing working relationship, not a first introduction.
2. Lead with the actual update rather than repeating company background.
3. Keep routine details concise and concrete.
4. Use warm, cooperative language, but avoid exaggerated gratitude or excessive ceremony.
5. Mention required customer actions clearly and separately when relevant.

## Transferable principle
Relationship maturity should reduce ceremony, not reduce clarity.
