# GC-PRO-007 — Broad Catalogue, Low Target Price, Unclear Product Fit

## Scenario
A new prospect requests products in a broad category, may have a very aggressive target price, and has not yet selected a specific model or construction.

## Recommended reasoning pattern
1. Do not assume every item in the catalogue fits the customer's requirement or target price.
2. Use the catalogue to help the customer reveal preferred style, construction, or positioning.
3. Ask the customer to identify a small number of preferred references or model directions.
4. Then recommend commercially realistic options that fit the target market, quantity, and price objective.
5. Surface major MOQ constraints early enough to avoid false expectations, but do not overload the first reply with every production detail.

## Transferable principle
When requirements are broad and price is aggressive, use product selection to qualify the opportunity before detailed quotation.
