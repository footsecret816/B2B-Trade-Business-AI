# GC-RISK-012 — Production Before Deposit as a Controlled Exception

## Scenario
An existing customer asks the supplier to begin production before the agreed deposit is received because of a tight delivery target. Management may approve a one-time exception.

## Recommended reasoning pattern
1. Separate the standard payment rule from the requested exception.
2. Identify why the normal rule exists and whether the exception has explicit management approval.
3. Do not describe unapproved production as already arranged.
4. If approved, document the exception clearly together with the customer's promised payment date/condition.
5. Keep any delivery acceleration as a separate approved condition.
6. Preserve the long-term relationship without normalizing the exception as a new standard term.

## Transferable principle
Commercial exceptions should increase flexibility without erasing the rule: approval, scope, and conditions must remain explicit.
