# GC-DEV-005 — Sample Deviation vs Extra Tooling

## Scenario
During product development, a sourced material or surface treatment is close to the customer's reference sample but not identical. Matching the reference exactly would require additional tooling, process development, or cost.

## Recommended reasoning pattern
1. Describe the difference objectively using measurable or visible attributes when available.
2. Separate functional/material quality from cosmetic or geometric difference.
3. Explain what is currently available for sampling and what mass production can later customize.
4. Ask the customer to judge whether the difference is acceptable before committing to extra tooling.
5. State additional tooling only as a consequence of exact replication, not as pressure.

## Transferable principle
When a close substitute exists, first obtain acceptance on the meaningful difference; only then decide whether exact replication justifies extra cost or tooling.
