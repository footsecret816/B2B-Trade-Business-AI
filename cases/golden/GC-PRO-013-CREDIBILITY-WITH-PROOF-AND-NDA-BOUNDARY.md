# GC-PRO-013 — Credibility Building with Proof and NDA Boundary

## Scenario
A new prospect needs confidence that the supplier can handle a meaningful OEM/private-label project. The supplier has concrete operational advantages and some customer references that may be publicly shareable, while other relationships are protected by confidentiality obligations.

## Recommended reasoning pattern
1. Lead with concrete, verifiable operational proof.
2. Use named customer references only when authorized or appropriate for external use.
3. If additional relationships are confidential, state the confidentiality boundary clearly.
4. Do not inflate capability with unsupported superlatives.
5. Connect credibility proof to the prospect's actual project need.

## Transferable principle
Credibility is strongest when it is concrete and scoped: show what can be proven, disclose only what is authorized, and tie the proof back to the customer's project.
