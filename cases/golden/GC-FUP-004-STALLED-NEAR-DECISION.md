# GC-FUP-004 — High-Value Project Stalled Near Decision

## Scenario
A project has required substantial time, negotiation, and internal effort. The commercial gap appears relatively small, but the customer has stopped replying for several days.

## Recommended reasoning pattern
1. Do not assume silence means rejection.
2. Recognize that the project history itself creates a legitimate reason to follow up.
3. Avoid a generic reminder with no new purpose.
4. Re-enter the conversation by expressing project concern, acknowledging how far both sides have progressed, and leaving a clear door open for final discussion.
5. If there may still be internal flexibility, refer to willingness to review rather than promising a concession.

## Strategy
Warm + low-pressure + reopen the decision.

## Transferable principle
For a mature project near decision, follow-up should reopen the business conversation, not merely remind the customer that an email was sent.
