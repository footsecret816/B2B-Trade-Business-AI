# EVAL-006 — Broad Prospect Qualification

## Test input
A new prospect requests a broad product category with an aggressive target price and no specific model selected.

## Required behaviors
- Do not imply the whole catalogue fits.
- Use product selection to narrow preferred style/construction.
- Recommend targeted follow-up rather than quoting everything.
- Surface major MOQ constraints when material.
- Do not reject solely because first target price looks low before construction is clarified.
