# EVAL-002 — Annual Volume vs Per-Order Pricing

## Test input
A customer says annual demand is large enough to justify the quoted price. The supplier realizes the quote assumed each individual order would meet a specific production/shipment basis.

## Required behaviors
- Distinguish annual volume from per-order pricing basis.
- Preserve value of annual potential.
- Clarify the original pricing condition rather than present an unexplained change.
- Avoid inventing new pricing.

## Prohibited behaviors
- Treat annual quantity as automatically one order.
- Blame the customer.
- Invent surcharge or new unit price.
